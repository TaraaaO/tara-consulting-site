HH
Tourism & Holiday Parks

Holiday Haven Tourist Parks

The situation

Holiday Haven manages twelve council-run parks across the NSW South Coast. Their social media presence was broad but inconsistent — content varied widely across parks with no unified brand voice, limited use of stories, and little collaboration between locations.

What we did

Took over full social media management across Facebook and Instagram — three posts per week plus stories. Built a consistent brand voice across all parks, introduced collaborative posts and shared stories between locations, and developed a content training resource to support park managers with local content ideas.

What changed

Content now feels cohesive across twelve parks. Stories are being used regularly to connect locations and share moments in real time. Park managers are more involved and confident contributing local content. We also identified early that promotional content underperforms for this audience — the focus shifted to personality-led and experience-based content, which consistently performs better.

Social media management Content strategy Brand consistency Training 12 months ongoing
AC
Early Childhood Education

ACEC

The situation

ACEC needed a social media presence that reflected their team and culture without being able to post photos of children or educators in a care setting. Content was sparse and not building the community recognition the centre deserved.

What we did

Developed a content approach centred on the faces and personalities of the educators themselves, industry-related content, and behind-the-scenes moments that bring the service to life without compromising privacy. Trialled different content types to find what resonated with their audience and what didn't.

What changed

The page now has a consistent presence that reflects the team's warmth and professionalism. We found quickly that promotional service content doesn't connect with this audience — what works is relatable, people-first content that makes families and educators feel seen. Google Business Profile work is also underway to strengthen their local visibility.

Social media content Content strategy Google Business Profile Early childhood
SD
Trades & Construction

South Coast Demolition

The situation

South Coast Demolition was operating without clear business systems — quoting was inconsistent, workflow was managed from memory, and the customer experience from first enquiry through to job completion wasn't structured in a way that scaled.

What we did

Built practical systems for quoting, job management and workflow tracking. Created a customer experience process that brings consistency to how enquiries are handled and how clients are communicated with throughout a job, from first contact to sign-off.

What changed

The business now runs on documented processes rather than individual memory. Quoting is consistent, jobs are tracked clearly, and the customer experience is more professional from start to finish. The systems are built to be used by the team, not just the owner.

Business systems Quoting process Workflow management Customer experience
DP
Heavy Plant & Equipment

Dom's Plant Repairs

The situation

Dom's Plant Repairs is a crane and heavy plant repair business based in Ingleburn, NSW. They had limited online visibility and no structured approach to marketing or internal organisation. The team needed a way to use modern tools without getting lost in complexity.

What we did

Set up and optimised their Google Business Profile to improve local search visibility. Introduced AI tools actively into the business to support marketing strategy, content creation and day-to-day organisation. Worked with the team to build their knowledge and confidence in using these tools independently.

What changed

The business now has a stronger online presence and a clearer approach to marketing. The team is using AI in practical ways that save time and reduce the back-and-forth on content and communications. They have the foundations to keep building on what's been set up without needing to outsource everything.

Google Business Profile AI implementation Marketing strategy Team training
